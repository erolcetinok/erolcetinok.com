How do I manage my money, and not go broke?! It's an age-old question for young adults, and especially for anyone edging closer to their entrance into the workforce, in the United States and everywhere else. My dad bought me this book and told me to read it, and I think it'll leave me on surer footing as I grapple with managing my own finances in the near future.
