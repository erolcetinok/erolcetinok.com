Whenever I walk through the Stanford quad, or anywhere at Stanford for that matter, I am in a state of awe. This state of awe is felt by many, the joggers who daily frequent the Stanford campus, the students (I'm guessing) lucky enough to attend, and of course, the tourists who flock to campus, rain or shine, to take pictures in front of the memorial church. Shortly put, the Stanford campus is world-renowned for it's beauty, and Paul V. Turner's book, albeit a ripe fifty years old at the time that I read it, perfectly captures the behind the scenes of what formed such a beautiful campus. His seminal book explores those behind its formation, The Founders... & the Architects (see what I did there ;). 

If you like architecture, or perhaps are a member of the large group who gawks at the Stanford campus whenever they are on it or see a photo of it, then this book is certainly for you. It explores the role and influence of many that came together to create the beginning of the university known far and wide today, including Leland Stanford, Jane Stanford, Fredrick Law Olmstead, and many more.

I highly recommend this book to anyone with the desire, even if its small, to learn about the architecture and creation of Stanford University.
