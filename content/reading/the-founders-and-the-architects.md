Whenever I walk through the Stanford quad I'm struck by its beauty, a feeling shared by students and tourists alike. Turner's book, though fifty years old by the time I read it, perfectly captures the story behind that beauty and the people who shaped it, from Leland and Jane Stanford to Frederick Law Olmsted. A must-read for anyone who has ever gawked at the Stanford campus and wondered how it came to be.
